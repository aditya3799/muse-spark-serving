Conclusion
Found useful public H200 layer-level profiles, a close 8xH200 serving recipe,
and public serving diagnostic methodology. Did not find a downloadable,
verified full-model Muse NVFP4 TP8/EP8 DFlash8 GPU timeline matching our pinned

lifetimes, with explicit attention to existing local enhancements and overlap.

already tested divisor changes as new work or revive a regressing expert port.

expert GEMM; guard short/long and full/sliding regimes separately.
Use the public data to choose experiments, not multiply independent speedups.

TTFT and serving metrics, and either an official result or a matching execution.
