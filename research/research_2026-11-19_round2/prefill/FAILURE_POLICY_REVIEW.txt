Independent review of coordination/projection_failure_policy, patch71f6152c

Read actual amended row/N invoke and registration implementations plus CPU fault
injection and private-index composition receipts. No numerical descriptor or
kernel changes. The status helper permits onlySUCCESS0/NOT_SUPPORTED15. Startup
registration votes fatal exceptions on the CPU group before acceptanceMIN; healthy
peer plans are closed on fatal vote. Recoverable Torch OOM coherently disables.
Runtime fatal statuses propagate to worker teardown rather than pretending native
fallback repaired an execution error. This composes with early74d63805 and row
no_grad07f4c518; those changes affect startup order and autograd lifetime only.

Residuals, not evidence of safe recovery:
- Row startup timing's vendor invocation is outside its allocation-error vote.
  Fatal dispatch therefore aborts this worker before gather; peers rely on existing
  worker/process failure teardown. The policy does not claim a hang-free recovery
  from a poisoned/asynchronously failing CUDA context.
- RowPlan's constructor takes the vendor handle then allocates scratch before
  discover enters its cleanup try. Torch OOM at that allocation can leak the
  newly prepared CABI handle. A guarded constructor call should destroy it before
  rethrowing; output-N's VendorPlan constructor does not allocate Torch scratch.
- The old row registration comment that unexpected discovery errors cause peers
  to fall back is stale: the new branch aborts on those errors.
- Factories remain static-checkpoint scope. This patch does not implement
  weight-reload invalidation, bound arbitrary new serving streams, or prove
  combined memory/serving behavior. Such claims require their separate gates.

Review is source/receipt only; no additional GPU, Gloo or NCCL execution here.
