Ring: dflash_draft_ring.py startup predicates explicitly reject FA3. MHA ring
backends are trtllm_mha/fa4; flashattention_backend.py973 also requires FA4,
plain MHA pool, no context parallel layout and topk1. An explicit compact draft
window is incompatible. Ring row sizing includes the attention window, verify
block and spare row/page to prevent live reads from being overwritten. The
pool and all writers/readers must share this new ownership map; changing only
one scatter is not a port.

Prefix ownership is a separate numerical constraint. A ring supplies no draft
KV in its HiCache plan (worker1161), records a floor equal to the first extend's
prefix-cache hit (ring349+), and clips reads below that floor. Native per-slot
draft cache can reuse those prefix rows. ring_applicability_cpu.json executes
seven actual upstream startup predicate cases and provides a simple equal-score
attention counterexample: native window8..15 versus ring floor12 reads12..15,
giving11.5 versus13.5. This is a logical counterexample, not a model measurement.
Verification could still preserve final greedy target tokens while changing
draft proposals, acceptance and work. It therefore does not satisfy the fixed
draft/verify execution policy without additional state reconstruction/proof.

L2 prefetch: dflash_draft_l2_prefetch.py selects only CUDA tensors no larger than
4MiB from non-Linear modules of each draft layer and final norm, holds strong
references, constructs a raw-address table, launches on a side stream after a
head dependency, and joins that stream at the draft graph tail. It does not
prefetch the large FC/LM-head/Linear weights. Default root model's actual list
and cache contention are not profiled. It is a possible small H200 cache-hint
experiment, not evidence of a material fraction or2x. Pointer lifetime/reload
retirement and capture hook compatibility would be mandatory before enabling.

The ring and prefetch audits do not modify frozen sampler/prologue packages.
