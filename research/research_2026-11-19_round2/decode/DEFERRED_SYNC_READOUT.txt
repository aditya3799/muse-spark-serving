Ordering evidence: pinned GroupCoordinator routes CUDA all-gather to PyNccl on
the current stream or synchronous Torch ProcessGroupNCCL; broadcast uses the
same current stream/native group. Pinned pynccl.py146,199,378 resolves current
device stream for both operations. PyTorch2.14 explicitly guarantees that
synchronous collective outputs can be used by subsequent operations on that
same CUDA stream. Thus local data dependencies do not require a second
broadcast. Removing one uniformly chosen collective also preserves the order
of all remaining collectives. This is a source argument, not measured evidence
about deployed multiple NCCL communicators, graph launch ordering, peer failure
or asynchronous allocator behavior. Actual multi-GPU stress/trace remains a
mandatory gate; no stronger ordering guarantee is claimed.
