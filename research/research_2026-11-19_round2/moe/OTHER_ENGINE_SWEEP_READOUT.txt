LMDeploy5142 (Nov16, open, head2e3592cf12d63b96d8adc27bb999fc92ace127f9)
and5173 (Nov19 11:59:58, open, head6f0dc62301d2e13a59499a95659877a4122450b4):
These are genuinely new NVFP4g16 engine integrations. They preserve packedE2M1
and avoid activation quantization, but the actual5173 registration is
Config_NVFP4<Sm80,half>, Family{kHalf,kHalf}; the source contradicts its PR prose
saying BF16compute. NVFP4Format.finalize divides block scales by the checkpoint
global and converts them toFP16, before the MMA. It therefore changes our
activation dtype and original global/rounding boundaries. Not a drop-in candidate.
SM75-only5142 does not changeSM80+ dispatch. 5161 is AWQ integerg128;5174 is
MXFP4g32/GPT-OSS and states it adds no new kernel code. Those do not provide our
NVFP4g16BF16 Hopper kernel. Exact config/format/registration leaves are archived.

FlashInfer9581 (Nov17, open, headfd6d8549c6bdac3d3a748663711fa1c1c1f9413c):
Real Hopper W4A16 code, but fixedE56/H3584/I3072/top16, MXFP4UE8M0 group32 and
SiTU. Multiple prefill output paths call atomicAdd on BF16/BF162 outputs.
Thus neither original format nor our FP32 combine contract matches. The PR's
4.08x weighted number is against Marlin on synthetic routing/tensors for another
model; it explicitly discloses L2-resident and decode-incumbent variance limits.
No transfer of that speedup to this task. Its resident cooperative decode grid,
stream-owned workspace and changed-input graph controls may inform future tests;
they do not establish Muse compatibility.

HuggingFace kernels-community1240 (createdOct16, stillopen; currenthead
cbd5b592734823dd0f9eab1fd006c41e1a912033, updated within this sweep):
Unlike the prior two, this source DOES have NVFP4g16 weight-only grouped Triton:
formats.py accepts E4M3 group16+rawBF16; mma.py::mx_weight_upcast unpacks FP4 and
multiplies the full group scale inBF16 before tl.dot. The scalar alternative
reduces inFP32 per group then applies group scale, so it has another reduction
order. grouped.py uses the same pure weight-only dispatcher for these formats.
However, gemm_epilogue applies global scale toFP32acc before BF16store/GLU, and
SIMULATE_UNFUSED has its own activation-rounding convention. This is not the
scored customHumming boundary. Reported benchmark results/optimizations areB200,
notHopper. Some paths are explicitlySM10x-scoped; ordinary dot/upcast has no
such hard gate in the inspected leaf, but a full Hopper backend/runtime test is
not performed. Its grouped/batched choice, swapped-weight small-token tiles and
path autotuner are secondary source leads, not evidence of a newH200speedup.
No downloaded implementation was imported or executed.

The strongest new eligible structural source remains SGLang50800, with separate
original, boundary-restored, and raw-scale-layout evidence. None of these sources
proves2x serving against the scoredHumming parent or authorizes a submission.
