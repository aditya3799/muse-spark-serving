MoE round 1 - 2026-11-16

Evidence and hypothesis
The scored compact32 change achieved 1.0111x serving. It already addresses
padded-route waste, so another fixed-small-M tile sweep is not justified.
The direct v139 persistent kernel has a large component improvement but its
current prepare retains eight full weight layouts across tiny/larger paths.
That cannot fit the <=2GB/GPU whole-model allowance.

Candidate experiment
The existing Marlin JIT source is within the allowed python/sglang tree:
python/sglang/kernels/jit/csrc/gemm/marlin_moe/moe_wna16_marlin.cuh.
Its determine_exec_config prioritizes the greatest resident CTA count among
two tile options. The persistent grid therefore trades occupancy against
split-K partial accumulation and barrier overhead without a latency model.
grid_experiment.py tests 1 and 2 CTA/SM caps against default, preserving the
chosen tile, canonical packed weights, FP32 reductions, and activation dtype.
It uses real existing Marlin kernels, activation and finalization. Variants
are isolated overlays, with no edits to the active checkout. It records
three changed-input/routing CUDA graph replays and cold-cache AB/BA samples.
A lower grid can regress compute throughput: no winner or gain is claimed.

Larger redesign feasibility
A WGS-only donor port could keep just its WGS byte layout, eliminate all
base/fat/unit/tiny copies, and use the same kernel for all tokens via chunks.
This would replace the canonical weight representation rather than retaining
another whole-model copy. The core WGS path appears to read only w[12/13]
plus per-expert scales/norm; host assignment of other pointer slots is not
proof that other layouts are needed. However, correctness requires adapting
stock BF16 global-scale rounding, gate/up scale collapse, effective norm
weight vs raw (1+w), supported epsilon/norm forms, local expert offsets,
changed/duplicate routing, and graph-safe shared scratch. PRMT scale decode
requires positive-normal FP8 scale checks and an actual fallback.
This is a multi-hour implementation, not currently a ready patch. No H200
hardware is available locally to execute its WGMMA/TMA path.
