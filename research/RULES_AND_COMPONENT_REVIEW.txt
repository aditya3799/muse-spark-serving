Live rules override our earlier init snapshot where they differ:
- 400 KB patch limit; added lines <=400 characters; source/JSON only under
  python/sglang; no model_loader changes, renames or binary changes.
- No lower precision, approximations or skipped work beyond stock SGLang.
- Extra GPU memory <=2 GB per GPU versus stock, including persistent repacks.
- Flags can change through the generated flags file except those listed fixed.
- TTFT <=2x stock at each load. Three checks and one scored run daily.
- Private serving board; maintainers participate in final review.

Promising transferable ideas:
- MoE: early first copy, warp-uniform completion polling, lean item transitions,
  warp-aggregated work claims and wide/narrow tile separation. The final MoE
  milestone reports 2.6315x in one judge run; it is a donor for study, not a
  performance promise. Its 639607-byte build also needs substantial extraction.
- Attention: compact modules and shorter launch/reduction chains, contiguous
  whole-KV-row loads, shorter cold instruction paths. Rework FP16-softmax or
  approximate EXPQ paths to match stock; do not import them blindly.
- Projections: reduce partial-output traffic, avoid load sinking into the tail,
  one-wave small GEMMs, exact escapes for small coefficients.
- Prefill: separate wide/narrow expert tiles, account for register contention
  between WGMMA and unpack work, avoid unsupported prefetch assumptions.
- Measurements: shared weights in A/B runs, multiple input placements, paired
  AB/BA timing and nearby shapes. The external dead ends overlap many local
  rejections; avoid repeating them without a new mechanism.

Implementation direction
Build a small, readable, default-safe SGLang patch with a stock fallback and
explicit GPU/layout checks. Start by auditing a compact MoE mechanism, with a
router-only change as a lower-integration-cost comparison. Choose actual tensor
geometry/workload features rather than matching concurrency 1/4/16/32. Include
memory accounting across every layer, correctness and end-to-end measurements.
Keep borrowed code's SPDX notices and required license/copyright attribution.
