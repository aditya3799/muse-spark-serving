Caches, repeated reads, splitK, activation formatconversion, transport and
different hardware rates prevent turning footprint fractions into runtime.

above matmuls; JSON includes2048/8192/74160 prefix scenarios. Firstprefill counts

and omitted draft/logit/transport work further limit scope, not prove speed.
