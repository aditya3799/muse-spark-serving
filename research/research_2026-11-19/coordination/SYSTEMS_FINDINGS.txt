4. Draft distribution correction: eight global draft KV heads mean each TP8 rank owns one distinct head. A simple all-gather of one head's packed KV is WRONG. Prefill lane invalidated that proposal. Safe current design gathers full FC projection output and retains original full-M RMS plus each rank's original KV. Alternative all-head local projection plus all-to-all would require extra weights,8separate unchanged-width GEMMs and independent ownership validation.

5. Lossless BF16 storage: Unweight, NF12, NWC and exponent-compression sources reviewed. Exact decoding of weights does not prove the GEMM's arithmetic or model outputs. Native full-decompression before original GEMM preserves weight bits but adds read/write traffic, so codec speed is not serving speed. Existing public H200 negative results already show12-bit decode loses atM128+ on several projections. NVFP4 coefficient entropy is close to4bits, so dense BF16 claims cannot be assigned to expert coefficients.
