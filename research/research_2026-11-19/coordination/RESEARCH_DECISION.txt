Prefill: owned-row projection passed actual-weight FC, normalization and

MoE: a native fixed-cycle loop passed two small positive local whole-FFN trials

Why the projection result does not establish 2x

Under a uniform Amdahl illustration, a 4.32x component would need to account for
about 49.7% of parent runtime to lift 1.2363x stock to 2x stock. That time share
has not been measured. Primary architecture accounting puts FC at roughly
3.6-5.8% of a limited matmul FLOP total; this is not a runtime percentage because
operators use different formats and achieve different efficiencies. It makes
projection alone a demanding hypothesis, not an impossibility proof.

Row and output-column variants accelerate the same FC at different shapes.
Their gains must not be multiplied. Cache gains and avoided recomputation must
also not be counted again as kernel gains. Combined patches require a fresh
peak-memory and full-workload review.

TTFT eligibility and normalized serving throughput remain decisive. Dummy
CUDA-graph profiles establish dispatch and shapes, not real workload time shares;

four-load frontier. No scored request follows from the current evidence.
