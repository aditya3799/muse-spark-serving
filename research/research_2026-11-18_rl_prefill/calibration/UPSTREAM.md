# Upstream Humming changes checked against the scored expert shard

The scored wrapper feeds BF16 activation and output, FP4 E2M1 weights with group-16 E4M3 scales, indexed sparse global routes and full-K FP32 accumulation. W13 uses K4096/N8192 and W2 uses K4096/N4096. The pinned Humming wheel is 0.1.12. Its SM86 and SM90 heuristics are independent subclasses of DeviceHeuristics.

* [PR 112](https://github.com/vllm-project/humming/pull/112/files) changes the call in this BF16 group-scale mainloop to dequant_scale with keep-dequant-order enabled. On SM86 both native conversion predicates are false. Its implementation then calls the same packed dequant<E4M3,BF16> present in the pin with no extra conversion. The removal of the previous explicit register permutation on this path came from [PR 107](https://github.com/vllm-project/humming/pull/107). The changed FP32 scale expansion serves other activation/scale paths. In the scored source, kDequantBSBits is 16 for A16 even when accumulation is FP32. There is no independent SM86 scale conversion to benchmark.
* [PR 213](https://github.com/vllm-project/humming/pull/213) published H200 indexed E32/top8 dimensions identical to the two scored GEMMs. Its E4M3 path requires native SM89+ group-scale conversion plus exponent-offset tracking absent from the pin. The author's FP4 W13/W2 geometric mean is 1.05–1.07× at 1–64 rows and 1.02–1.04× at 256–16,384 rows against upstream main. Of 360 reported precision comparisons, 12 down-projection cases at M4 differed bitwise. It cannot activate on this RTX 3050. These figures cover GEMMs only.
* [PR 152](https://github.com/vllm-project/humming/pull/152/files) adds a weight eviction rule to Sm80Heuristics and its SM87 subclass. Neither active architecture inherits this class. Its A100 block-FP8 kernel measurements showed a small improvement, and its serving result was within 1% of Marlin.
* [PR 156](https://github.com/vllm-project/humming/pull/156) requires K ≤ 768. [PR 175](https://github.com/vllm-project/humming/pull/175) requires grouped contiguous E8M0/group-32 MXFP4; the quoted 1.77–1.79× is the W4A8 case. [PR 159](https://github.com/vllm-project/humming/pull/159) targets dense short-K decode. [PR 52](https://github.com/vllm-project/humming/pull/52) enables FP32 Stream-K workspace on H20 indexed MXFP4A8 with fused E8M0 scales. [PR 198](https://github.com/vllm-project/humming/pull/198) changes launch-time prefetch depth, with mixed H20 numbers around a few percent; no matching indexed NVFP4 benefit is reported.

No independent upstream variant met both local qualification and transfer requirements. No GPU test or submission followed this pass.
