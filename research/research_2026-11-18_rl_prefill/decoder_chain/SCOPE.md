# Integrated decoder workload

The official parent scored 1.2363× over 13 turns, a mean 74,160-token first turn, 753 added tokens per subsequent turn and mean 220 generated tokens. It recorded 0.92 cache hit and 4.0–4.1 acceptance at all four loads. First-turn p90 rose from 6.536 to 134.930 seconds between 1 and 32 users. In the approximate phase reconstruction, generation accounts for 59%, 58%, 47% and 30% at 1, 4, 16 and 32 users. The earlier H200 Hopper expert trial passed numerical checks but regressed C16 first-turn p90 from 61.385 to 174.256 seconds. The present rank-chain trial measures verification only. The local plan targets B1 with up to 74,160 positions, B4 up to 8,192 and B16 up to 4,096. The latter lengths permit four independent real K/V banks on a six GiB card. Live lengths vary within the upper half of each range. Each block owns distinct dense projections, router weights and history. One scored-format resident expert bank represents rank zero of EP8 and is reused across all four blocks. O and finalized-expert all-reduces use the single local partial. Before each replay, peer projection columns are recomputed using the current live reference inputs. Timed operations calculate the local 1/8 projection columns. An identical new-row E4M3-to-BF16 bridge in both arms feeds native Ampere paged attention. This preserves E4M3 entry and real paged attention computation with a different inner attention precision.

The candidate uses the qualified E4M3 packet entry and local-window scheduler reuse. At M8/M32 it executes the exact same scored Humming FFN. Only M128 uses the independent grouped expert candidate. The default retains private page tables, selected after the 62-layer attention screen.
