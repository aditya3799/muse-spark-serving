# W13 route-grouped scale and SiLU

Scored Humming W13 writes BF16 gate/up rows, applies a BF16 per-expert scale in place, rereads the results, then computes the SiLU product. The proposed CUDA kernel ballots eight adjacent routing slots and works only on local experts. Its one pass preserves both BF16 input roundings, uses the pinned CUDA SiLU implementation and flags, and leaves inactive output slots untouched. The complete candidate applies to SGLang commit 297e54fd and the scored 1.2363x Humming parent, SHA256 8c7547213fb993a318321a26f84e2872db8c87b358ee3d3ea638695702b0dc2b. Its own SHA256 is 33a0a65719afafe691e5c685a7a32ead3194112fc18ce8434f3b31880ce9a721.

On the RTX 3050 6GB, all six complete output buffers matched the parent bit-for-bit across 1,320,484,864 BF16 values. Two independent seeds each had twelve alternating pairs and a separate unchanged-parent graph. Pilot ratios were 2.274x at 753 rows, 2.364x at 3,012 and 2.293x at 16,384. The independent rates were 2.284x, 2.363x and 2.297x. Across the three equal-weight shapes the benchmark plugin returned 2.311x (2.307-2.317) and 2.314x (2.313-2.316). Numerical checks use varying BF16 expert scales. Repeated timing calls use unit scales because the parent writes its input in place. The data are synthetic sparse routing fixtures and a clustered stress case.

The scored H200 Humming component profile at 16,384 query rows and a 32,768-token sliding prefix assigned 284.2 microseconds to the activation kernel (2.6% of listed kernel time). This historical H200 component differs from the scored serving graph. A broader local routed-expert comparison incorporating alignment, W13, activation, W2 and finalization found 1.023x on the active-shape pilot with a noisy duplicate control, then 1.019x on its holdout. The 16,384 case in that wider test is four 4096-row dispatches. The isolated activation improvement is reproducible; the broader experiment did not pass two controlled promotion gates.

`candidate.diff` packages the entire candidate. `delta.diff` applies to the scored parent. `pilot.json` and `holdout.json` contain paired microseconds, order and hashes. `offline.log` records SM90a compilation at 32 registers and zero spills. H200 runtime timing and serving-token parity are unmeasured. The independent main-GEMM route staging experiment is under `gemm/`.
