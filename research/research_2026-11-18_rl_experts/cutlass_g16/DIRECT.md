# Small-row canonical FP4 loading through the frozen Humming pipeline

The two real native fallback seeds established exact output bits after selective Humming-layout restoration. All changed-input CUDA graph replays at 8/32 target rows matched every live raw W2 and finalized BF16 value. The complete rank-local expert path slowed to 0.276–0.449× baseline on RTX. Restoring even the sole selected expert cost about 11.4 ms at these local clocks. Stored duplicate controls closely tracked the original parent. These results motivate loading the exclusive bank directly inside Humming. [Pilot](fallback_canonical_31079.json) [Holdout](fallback_canonical_77161.json)

The direct alternative replaces the B and weight-scale global-to-shared loads in the private ordinary Humming kernel. It reconstructs each native shared word from the canonical bank as its CTA consumes it. The parent's MMA tile, accumulator/reduction order, scaling, activation and finalizer remain intact. Its dispatch requires indexed BF16/FP4, group size 16, no Stream-K, no separate warp producer, no TMA or mbarrier, and no multicast. The existing consumer synchronizes all CTA threads after its async-copy wait, which makes the new ordinary shared-memory stores visible. Other configurations use the byte-verified native restorer. All-direct forwards avoid both the weight repack and the active-expert histogram. The original and changed native W13/W2 kernels compiled for both SM86 and SM90a. At the fixture geometries the SM86 W13/W2 register count rose from 120 to 128, and SM90a W13/W2 fell from 85/87 to 80. All eight original/candidate compilations had zero local memory and zero stack bytes. The same C++ producer bodies were executed by a CPU compiler and matched 589,824 native tile bytes, including nonzero K-, N- and expert offsets, both mini-transposes and every scale encoding. This proves word addressing at the covered geometries. Runtime capture, raw output equivalence, and local/H200 latency still require their respective hardware trials. The direct implementation saves a full memory round trip at the cost of gathering canonical bytes inside each CTA. The source-level 32-lane check found 16 distinct 32-byte sectors per four byte gathers, versus 4 per contiguous parent warp word load. Repeated accesses can use cache. Production activation therefore requires measured evidence at the original kernel geometry. Local study entry point is `qualify_fallback.py --direct --seed 31079 --rows 8 32 --rounds 4`, with independent holdout `77161`. These experiments have no external submission hooks.
