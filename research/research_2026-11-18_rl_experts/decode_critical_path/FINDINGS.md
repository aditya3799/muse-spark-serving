# H200 decode critical path

The public whole-layer trace and our separate S081 profile exercise the same 80K, page-one, per-rank H200 decoder task. The task weights 46 sliding layers and 16 full layers per step. Both traces measure single-rank kernels with identity collectives. The official 1.2363× serving run uses eight GPUs and reports initial prompts averaging 74,160 tokens, 753 new tokens per turn, and four client concurrencies. Its receipt gives end-to-end throughput and no per-kernel trace. B4 has no matching kernel-board cell. ## Two investigations of full-context KV traffic

**Native paged TMA eligibility.** The pinned FA3 Hopper code requires an integral page-size multiple of the KV tile and effective query length strictly greater than its Q tile. The public model's TP rank has 16 query heads and 2 KV heads. Decode/width-eight verify supply effective lengths 8/64. The small-Q policy already selects one MMA warpgroup with a 64-row Q tile. At width eight the strict condition still fails. Even an allocator that supplied contiguous 128-token ranges could not activate the stock TMA path at these shapes. [FA3 gate](../../swarm_2026-11-16/attention_speculation/phase2/swa_scheduler_reuse/native_sources/flash_api.cpp) · [Dispatch](../../swarm_2026-11-17/deep_research/coordination/fa3_policy/heuristics.h) · [Tile geometry](../../swarm_2026-11-17/deep_research/coordination/fa3_policy/tile_size.h)

**Cross-request shared-prefix reads.** We used the Muse tokenizer and counted the exact duplicate prefix nodes in cohorts from 20 Muse, 491 OpenHands and 40 SWE-smith requests. At B32, duplicate prefix reads represented 58.5%, 57.8% and 8.5% of the visible short initial inputs. Preserving those prefixes and adding distinct private suffixes to the official 74,160-token mean reduced their optimistic full-context duplicate read shares to 0.844%, 1.508% and 0.071%. B1 always has zero cross-request reuse. Permuting cohort order had little effect. This is an input-sharing ceiling for the declared stress. Actual private judge prefixes, physical sharing and DRAM misses are unknown. The kernel-board generator assigns disjoint physical pages across requests; it offers no shared-prefix opportunity. No end-to-end H200 gain can be inferred for the local entry, page, sampler or unpool changes. Parameterizing their aggregate effect requires their separate H200 durations, true model role and invocation counts, collective exposure, and the number of accepted tokens per serving iteration.
